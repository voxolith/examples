Examples

Small, self-contained pages showing the @voxolith/renderer API. Each one is a single main.ts.

  1. hello-vox — load a .vox, build the occupancy grid, render with an auto-rotating camera
  2. orbit — procedural terrain, drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, floor plane
  3. minecraft-region — drop a Minecraft .mca region and walk through it first-person
  4. world — a generated valley: terrain and a river with animated water, a village and a forest that fade into each other, a lamp to carry and a day/night toggle
  5. valley — the same valley at 100 voxels per metre, a rat's-eye scale: refined models drawn as instances, the ground streamed in chunks, real-size rats; ?variants=, ?rats=, ?radius= (metres)
  6. creature — rigged, animated rats from the creature generator: tap one to wound it (fur opens onto flesh and bone), tap again to take a limb off
  7. swarm — hundreds of animated rats sharing a pose cache and a budgeted crowd stamper; hover or tap to scatter them, ?count= for more, with a live cost overlay
  8. instances — scenery and a crowd drawn by reference: six tree models placed at any heading, rats gliding and turning smoothly, nothing written into the world as they move; ?stamp stamps the trees instead, for comparison

Needs a WebGPU browser and a secure context. Source: github.com/voxolith/examples.

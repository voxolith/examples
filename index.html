Examples

Small, self-contained pages showing the @voxolith/renderer API. Each one is a single main.ts.

  1. hello-vox — load a .vox, build the occupancy grid, render with an auto-rotating camera
  2. orbit — procedural terrain, drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, floor plane
  3. minecraft-region — drop a Minecraft .mca region and walk through it first-person
  4. forest — a whole scene from the entity generators: trees, shrubs and ground cover sharing one palette
  5. village — houses on levelled ground joined by footpaths, woods and stones around them, all generated

Needs a WebGPU browser and a secure context. Source: github.com/voxolith/examples.
